When you fill out our enquiry or registration form, we may collect the following personal details:

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties, advertisers, or external organizations. Your information remains strictly within Agrawal Classes and is used only for the purposes stated in this policy.

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information you provide to us. Access to your data is limited to authorised staff at Agrawal Classes who require it to assist you.
